# Bridal OS — 5-Minute Demo Script

> **The one sentence:** *"A bride buys a dress. Bridal Live keeps handling the sale, inventory, and order. Bridal OS takes over everything the bride sees from that moment until pickup."*

This demo sells **one emotional workflow**, not a feature tour. Lead with the
bride portal — it's the strongest asset. Don't explain the dashboard plumbing.

---

## Before the call (2 min of setup)

1. Be logged in to a clean boutique account on the live URL.
2. Have the **Settings** already filled: store name (use the boutique's real
   name if you know it), logo, brand color. A personalized portal lands harder.
3. Have your phone ready to open the bride portal link (the portal is the
   "wow" — show it on a phone, that's how brides see it).
4. Know the boutique's name and that they use **Bridal Live**. Say it out loud
   in the first 30 seconds.

---

## The framing line (say this first, every time)

> "This is **not** a replacement for Bridal Live. Keep Bridal Live for your
> point of sale, inventory, and special orders. Bridal OS is the layer on top
> that handles everything the *bride* sees after she says yes to the dress —
> her fittings, payments, documents, and what's next. Let me show you."

---

## The sample bride story

Use a believable local story. Default persona:

- **Sienna Martinez**, wedding **~6 months out**.
- Dress: **designer gown, $3,500 total, $1,500 deposit paid**, $2,000 balance.
- Needs: a **first fitting**, a **hem alteration**, a **final balance payment**,
  and her **signed contract** on file.

This mirrors the built-in sample data, so it's safe to demo live.

---

## The 5-minute flow

### 1. Boutique adds the bride (45 sec)
- Click **Add new bride**.
- Enter: name (*Sienna Martinez*), email, wedding date, total price ($3,500),
  paid amount ($1,500).
- **Say:** "This is the only place I re-enter anything — name, date, balance.
  Thirty seconds. Bridal Live still has the order; this is just her timeline."

### 2. Build her timeline (90 sec)
- Open her **Details** sheet.
- **Dress tab:** add designer, style, size, color.
- **Appointments tab:** add a **First Fitting** next week. (Note the live
  availability check warns on conflicts.)
- **Alterations tab:** add **"Hem 2 inches – Maria"**, $150.
- **Documents tab:** upload the contract PDF, toggle **"Show to bride."**
- **Say:** "Everything about this bride now lives in one place instead of a
  spreadsheet, three sticky notes, and a group text."

### 3. Send the portal (30 sec)
- Click **Portal link** on her card → copy.
- **Say:** "This is the link the bride gets. No password, no app to download."

### 4. THE MOMENT — open the bride portal on a phone (90 sec)
- Open the link on your phone and turn the screen to them.
- Walk through what *she* sees:
  - Her name + a **countdown to the wedding**.
  - Her **journey timeline** (where she is, what's next).
  - **Next appointment** — and she can **request** a different time.
  - **Measurements** she can **confirm** with one tap.
  - Her **payment schedule** with a **Pay Now** button (Stripe).
  - Her **documents** to download.
- **Say:** "This is the difference. Your bride stops calling to ask 'when's my
  fitting' and 'how much do I still owe' — it's all right here, branded as you."

### 5. Close the loop (45 sec)
- Back on the dashboard, show the **appointment request inbox** and the
  **pulse / balances** view.
- **Say:** "Fewer calls, fewer 'did she pay' questions, nothing falling through
  the cracks between the sale and the pickup. That's the whole product."

## What to say vs. what NOT to claim

### Say (all true today)
- "Works **alongside** Bridal Live — it doesn't touch your POS or inventory."
- "Each bride gets a private, branded portal — no login required."
- "Fittings, alterations, payment schedules, reminders, and documents in one place."
- "Automated email reminders for appointments and payments."
- "Pay online through Stripe; it marks itself paid automatically."
- "Set up in an afternoon."

### Do NOT claim (not built / not validated)
- ❌ Any **specific metric** — no "98% engagement," "60% less admin,"
  "32% fewer no-shows." Say "boutiques tell us…" only when one actually has.
- ❌ **Multi-location**, **team roles/permissions**, **custom workflows** —
  on the roadmap, not built. If asked: "That's on the roadmap — what would you need?"
- ❌ **Importing from / syncing with Bridal Live** — there is **no** integration.
  Data entry is manual or CSV. Be explicit: "It runs beside Bridal Live, it
  doesn't sync with it."
- ❌ **SMS / texting** — email only today.
- ❌ **SOC2 / certifications** — say "secure, token-based portals," nothing more.
- ❌ Inventory, accounting, or POS features — that's Bridal Live's job, on purpose.

---

## If they ask "does this replace Bridal Live?"

> "No — and we don't want it to. Bridal Live is great at the sale, inventory,
> and orders. Bridal OS is the bride-facing layer on top. They run side by side."

## If they ask "how is this different from a CRM?"

> "A generic CRM doesn't know what a fitting or an alteration is, and it
> definitely doesn't give your bride a portal. This is built for the months
> between 'yes to the dress' and pickup."

---

## The ask at the end

> "We're taking on **5 pilot boutiques**. It's **free for 30 days**, we set it
> up with you, and all we want back is an honest call telling us whether this
> actually solves the post-sale chaos. Want to be one of them?"
